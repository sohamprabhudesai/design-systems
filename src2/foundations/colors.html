background: oklch(var(--avatar-l) var(--avatar-c) <hue>), color: var(--avatar-fg), where hue = cyrb53(seed) % 360 and seed = user id, else lower-cased email, else name. Light 60% / 0.15, dark 55% / 0.13. Without a seed the fallback is --muted / --muted-foreground.
