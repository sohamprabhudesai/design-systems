Dialogs, Sheet & Menus

Overlay surfaces share one recipe: --popover background and a 1px foreground/10 ring instead of a border. Dialog & Alert are 14px-radius boxes with 24px titles and a muted/50 footer band (384 / 512 / 768). Sheets are 384 / 512 / 672 with a left-aligned muted footer. Menus get shadow-lg and 36px rows; Tooltip is --foreground.
