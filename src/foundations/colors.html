Colors

All colors are exposed as RGB-triplet CSS variables (--neeto-ui-gray-800: 16, 24, 40) and consumed as rgb(var(--neeto-ui-gray-800)). Grays carry content and structure; primary (green) is reserved for primary actions and selected states; accent (blue) for links and informational highlights.
