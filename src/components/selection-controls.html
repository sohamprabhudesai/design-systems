Selection Controls

Components: Checkbox, Radio, Switch. Checked color is always primary-500; unchecked borders are gray-400 (2px). Labels are 14px medium, 8px from the control (12px for Switch).

Checkbox — 16px, radius 3px

Radio — 16px, horizontal by default, stacked variant

Switch — 44 × 24px track, 20px knob

Use Switch for instant on/off settings; Checkbox for form consent/multi-selection; Radio for 2–5 mutually exclusive options (else use Select).
